JDH – Jiwi Daah Hasa

Digital platform for managing and monitoring the Jharkhand programme and related spatial data

Core System Capabilities

Key modules developed for spatial monitoring and administrative reporting

  • Geographical Data Structure: Multi-tier drill-down across State → District → Block → Village.
  • GIS / Map Integration: Spatial thematic layers, watershed boundaries, and interactive maps.
  • Programme & DPR Management: Detailed Project Report tracking, work status, and progress monitoring.
  • Analytical Dashboards: Automated reporting engines and data export utilities.

Application Demonstration #3

SAVYA – Natural Farming Platform

Digital platform for collecting and managing information related to natural farming, participating households & plot data

Core System Capabilities

End-to-end field data collection, beneficiary management, and monitoring workflows

  • Digital Field Data Collection: Structured forms and mobile-friendly data intake.
  • Household & Farmer Profiling: Beneficiary demographics, landholding details, and crop history.
  • Farm & Plot Geo-Tagging: Plot level boundaries, crop practices, and input usage.
  • Activity & Yield Monitoring: Tracking adoption of natural farming practices and outcome metrics.
